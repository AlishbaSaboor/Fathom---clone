## Making the 8-person call differ in shape

I'd make it use the model differently:

- **Chapters:** a 6 to 8 entry `chapters` array, which drives a jump-to rail in the Transcript tab. The small calls have none.
- **Action items:** 15 to 20, versus 3 to 5 in the small calls. They use `group`, `dueDate` and `priority`, so the list renders grouped by workstream instead of as one flat list.
- **Summary:** it gets an extra "Decisions" section. Its `topics` section has many timestamped entries.
- **Highlights:** 4 to 5, versus 1 in the small calls.
- **Transcript:** about 120 to 160 segments with 8 distinct speakers. Turns vary from one-liners to long paragraphs, with interruptions and short acknowledgements.

The components must handle both shapes: group headers only appear when items have a `group`, and the chapter rail only appears when `chapters` exists. A flat 5-item list and a grouped 18-item list will both need to look right.

## Decisions for you

1. **Template summaries: stored or derived?** I recommend storing them. Each meeting has hand-written sections for General, Sales and Standup, so switching visibly changes the sections (for example, Standup uses the `byPerson` layout) and one generic renderer handles all of them. Real Fathom re-runs an LLM per template, so stored output is the closer approximation.
2. **`speakerId` instead of a speaker name string.** Speakers and assignees reference `Attendee.id`, so names, avatars and colors stay consistent and a rename only happens in one place. It's a small deviation from your "speaker" wording.
